## 1. Reddit r/SideProject 帖（首发主力帖）

**标题：**
I built a free lottery number picker with a "birthday mode" — no signup, no ads

**正文：**
I built LuckyPick (https://lucky.daobox.app) because picking lottery numbers with my family was always the same boring conversation: "just do Quick Pick again?"

So I made a few fun ways to pick:

- 🎂 Birthday Mode — turns your birthday into a number set
- 🔢 LUCKY FACTORS — picks numbers from your lucky number's factors
- ♈ Zodiac Mode — 12 sign-based picks
- 🎲 Classic random, if that's your thing

Honest disclaimer up front: **it doesn't improve your odds** — nothing can. It just makes picking numbers fun and personal. No signup, no ads, works right in the browser.

It's free. There's a paid Pro version (saved picks, extra modes) if you find it useful, but the core tool costs nothing.

Would love feedback on the modes — especially whether Birthday Mode feels fun or gimmicky.
