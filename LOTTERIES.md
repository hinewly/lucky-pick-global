# 4 Lotteries Reference

> LuckyPick Global supports 4 international lotteries. This doc explains the rules
> and how each game is played, for reference.

## Quick Comparison

| Game | Region | Main Balls | Extra Balls | Draw Days | Ticket Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Powerball** | 🇺🇸 US | 5 from 1-69 | 1 Powerball from 1-26 | Mon, Wed, Sat | $2 |
| **Mega Millions** | 🇺🇸 US | 5 from 1-70 | 1 Mega Ball from 1-25 | Tue, Fri | $2 |
| **EuroMillions** | 🇪🇺 EU | 5 from 1-50 | 2 Lucky Stars from 1-12 | Tue, Fri | €2.50 |
| **UK Lotto** | 🇬🇧 UK | 6 from 1-59 | 1 Bonus Ball from 1-59 | Wed, Sat | £2 |

All 4 are "5+1" or "5+2" or "6+1" format — **NOT "6+1"** in the same way.
The Chinese 双色球 ("6+1" = 6 red 1-33 + 1 blue 1-16) is a different game.

## 1. 🇺🇸 Powerball

**Website**: https://www.powerball.com/  
**Schedule**: Monday, Wednesday, Saturday — ~10:59 PM ET  
**Ticket**: $2 per line  
**Add-on**: $1 Power Play (multiplies non-jackpot prizes)

### How to Play

Pick **5 numbers** from 1 to 69 (white balls), plus **1 Powerball** number
from 1 to 26 (red ball). Total: 5+1 = 6 numbers selected.

### How the Draw Works

Two separate machines:
- Machine 1 contains 69 white balls — 5 are drawn
- Machine 2 contains 26 red balls — 1 is drawn

You win if your numbers match the drawn numbers. **The order does not matter.**

### Prize Tiers (without Power Play)

| Match | Prize (approx.) |
|---|---|
| 5 white + Powerball | **Jackpot** ($20M+ to $2B+) |
| 5 white (no Powerball) | $1,000,000 |
| 4 white + Powerball | $50,000 |
| 4 white (no Powerball) | $100 |
| 3 white + Powerball | $100 |
| 3 white (no Powerball) | $7 |
| 2 white + Powerball | $7 |
| 1 white + Powerball | $4 |
| Powerball only | $4 |
| Other | $0 |

Overall odds of winning any prize: ~1 in 25.

## 4. 🇬🇧 UK Lotto

**Website**: https://www.national-lottery.co.uk/lotto  
**Schedule**: Wednesday, Saturday — ~8:00 PM GMT  
**Ticket**: £2 per line

### How to Play

Pick **6 numbers** from 1 to 59. A 7th "Bonus Ball" is drawn separately
from the same 1-59 pool. Total: 6 main + 1 bonus = 7 numbers drawn per game,
but you only pick 6.

This is the only one of the 4 games where you pick **6 main balls** instead of 5.

### How the Draw Works

Two machines:
- Machine 1: 59 balls — 6 drawn as main balls
- Machine 2: same 59 balls — 1 drawn as bonus ball

### Prize Tiers

| Match | Prize (approx.) |
|---|---|
| 6 main | **Jackpot** (£2M+, can roll over £50M+) |
| 5 main + bonus | £1M+ |
| 5 main | £1,750 |
| 4 main | £140 |
| 3 main | £30 |
| 2 main | Free Lotto Lucky Dip ticket |

Overall odds of winning any prize: ~1 in 10 (highest of the 4 games).

## Common Misconceptions

### "It's all 6+1"
- ✗ Wrong for any of the 4 games (each has its own format)
- ✓ Powerball/MM = **5+1** = 6 balls drawn total
- ✓ EuroMillions = **5+2** = 7 balls drawn total
- ✓ UK Lotto = **6+1** = 7 balls drawn total

### "More tickets = better odds"
- Mathematically true but expensive
- 100 tickets = 100× odds, still ~1 in 3M for jackpot
- "量力而行" (live within your means) — don't spend more than you can afford to lose

### "Lucky numbers help"
- The app weights picks based on personal factors for **entertainment value only**
- All combinations have identical mathematical odds
- Past draws do NOT influence future draws (each is independent)

### "You can buy tickets from outside the US"
- ✗ For US lotteries (PB/MM), tickets must be bought in person at a US retailer
- ✓ Third-party courier services exist but are gray-area legally
- ✓ EuroMillions/UK Lotto require being in a participating country
