Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Overview

LuckyPick (https://lucky.daobox.app) is designed to work entirely in your browser. We do not require accounts and we do not collect personal information on our servers.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your saved number picks, preferences, and (if you purchase Pro) your license key are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device except as described below, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser storage.

3. Payments

Pro purchases are processed by Paddle (paddle.com), our Merchant of Record. When you buy Pro, Paddle collects your payment details and email address to process the transaction and deliver your license key. We do not receive or store your payment card information. Paddle's handling of your data is described in Paddle's own privacy policy.

4. Tracking and Analytics

LuckyPick does not use advertising cookies, third-party trackers, or analytics services.

5. Children

LuckyPick is intended for users aged 18 and over.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
