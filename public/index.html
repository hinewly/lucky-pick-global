A specific date (your birthday, anniversary). Its digits get pulled into the mix.
♈ Zodiac Sign
Pick your Western zodiac sign. Adds that sign\'s traditional lucky numbers.
💭 Dream Symbol
Type one or more dream keywords (e.g. water cat bird), max 256 characters. Each keyword has its own number set.
🔢 Life Path Number
Enter your birthday. Calculates your numerology Life Path number, then mixes it in.
🎵 Lyric / Phrase
Type any lyric, sentence, or thought (max 256 characters). Generates a fresh set of numbers each Generate.

💡 Why these 7 factors?

The 6 + 1 design — and how to use them.

6 input factors
Lucky #, Avoid #, Birthday, Zodiac, Dream, Life Path — each one is a clear number or pick you control.
1 free-text factor
Lyric / Phrase — type anything (a lyric, a thought, a sentence). It gets hashed into a fresh number set on every Generate.
Use 0, 1, some, or all
Want pure random? Skip everything. Want maximum personal touch? Add as many as you like. There's no required minimum.
Saved locally, always private
Everything you add is stored in your browser's localStorage on this device only. No signup, no server, no tracking. Close the tab — your factors are still here next time.

Install LuckyPick ↓

Once installed, just tap the icon on your home screen — it opens like a regular app, works offline.

🍎
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome or other browsers)
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Name it "LuckyPick" → tap Add
  5. Look for the icon on your home screen
🤖
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open this page in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right)
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"
  4. Confirm → look for the icon on your home screen

💡 Once installed, you don't need a browser. The app works offline.
💡 Updates happen automatically — just open the app and pull to refresh (if supported).
