📱 Add to your phone? (so you don't open a browser every time) Click to expand

You can install LuckyPick as an app on your phone. Once installed, just tap the icon on your home screen — it opens like a regular app, works offline.

🍎
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome or other browsers)
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Name it "LuckyPick" → tap Add
  5. Look for the icon on your home screen
🤖
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open this page in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right)
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"
  4. Confirm → look for the icon on your home screen

💡 Once installed, you don't need a browser. The app works offline.
💡 Updates happen automatically — just open the app and pull to refresh (if supported).
